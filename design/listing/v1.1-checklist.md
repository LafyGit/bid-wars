## In the app (done in code)

- Interstitial after a finished round (on "See round awards"), skipped for the first round and at most once every 150 s.
- Rewarded ad on the paywall when it was opened from a locked topic: watch to play that topic this sitting.
- Any purchase turns all ads off. Paywall copy says so.
- Consent: Google UMP first, then Apple's tracking prompt; declining gives non-personalised ads. Ads max rating PG.
- Debug builds always use Google test units; release builds show nothing until real unit IDs are filled in.
