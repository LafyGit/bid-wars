PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: Bid Wars does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The names you type for the two players, the match score, your game statistics, your settings and a record of which topic packs you own are saved on your phone only, so the game can remember them next time. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Optional topic packs are sold as in-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app only learns, from Apple, which purchases are on your account so it can unlock them.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for an email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • We do not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children. Topic packs contain only plain-text names of games, shows, foods, places and similar everyday subjects.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/LafyGit/bid-wars/issues.
