BID WARS

Blind Auction: a two-player party game for one phone.

How it works. Two players start with $20 of fake money and bid back and forth on ten hidden items from a topic you pick. Raise by at least $1 or pass. Nobody can win more than five items. When the last item is gone, compare collections and decide who won.

Frequently asked

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, or open any locked topic and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

What do the purchases unlock?

Bid Wars is free to play with a large set of free topic packs. Group packs (Gaming, Sports & Cars, Screen & Music, Food & Travel, Tech & Lifestyle) unlock every pack in that group. Bid Wars Pro unlocks every pack, including packs added later. All purchases are one-time and are not subscriptions.

Is real money involved in the game?

No. All bidding uses fake money with no cash value. There are no prizes and no gambling.
